Privacy Policy

youtube-lab is a personal tool used only by its owner.

What data is accessed

With the owner's permission, the tool reads statistics of the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Analytics API and the YouTube Data API, using read-only access. It does not access any other user's account.

The tool also reads comments on public YouTube videos through the YouTube Data API. It receives only the comment text, the date and time it was posted, the number of likes, and the number of replies, and the owner reads them as reference for planning videos. It does not receive any information that identifies the person who wrote a comment, such as their name, channel, or profile image, and the received data is deleted within 30 days.

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only for the owner's own purposes: reviewing the performance of their own videos and planning new ones. The data is not sold. At the owner's direction, it may be processed by AI services that help the owner plan videos (Anthropic, Google, and OpenAI). It is not provided to any other person or company. Among these services, only Anthropic's Claude processes the comment text itself; the other AI services receive only short summaries that the owner wrote in their own words, which do not include any information that identifies the person who wrote a comment. All of these services are used with settings under which the submitted content is not used to train their models. The data exists only on the owner's own computer and within the processing of these services.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page. After access is revoked, the stored data is deleted within 7 days.
